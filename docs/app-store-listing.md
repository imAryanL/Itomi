## Description (4000 max)

Itomi is a row and stitch counter made for crochet, and for amigurumi most of all. Tap anywhere to count, and Itomi saves after every tap, so you never lose your place.

MADE FOR AMIGURUMI
• Split a project into parts like Head, Body and Ears ×2, each with its own counter
• See which pieces are done and which are left at a glance
• When a part is finished, Itomi takes you straight to the next one
• When every part is done, tick off the sewing-up steps: stuff, sew on, embroider the face

NEVER LOSE YOUR PLACE
• One big tap area for rows and rounds, plus a stitch counter
• Undo, even after closing the app
• Saved after every single tap
• Come back after days away and pick up right where you stopped

CALM AND RESPECTFUL
• No ads, no account, no sign-up
• Big, clear numbers and large buttons
• Your projects stay on your phone, and you can back them up any time for free

FREE, FOREVER
• Projects with photo, hook or needle size, yarn and notes
• Counter with rows, stitches and undo
• Parts, the assembly checklist and the Finished shelf
• Backup and restore
• 2 active projects at a time (finished projects don't count)

ITOMI PRO
• Counter on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
• Count with Siri, the Action Button or a Control Center button
• Stitch-count warning: set the stitches for each round and Itomi tells you when a round doesn't add up
• Unlimited active projects
• Six yarn-colored themes and matching app icons
• Make another: start a fresh copy of a finished project with the same parts and rounds

Pro is $12.99 a year with a free first week, or $29.99 once for lifetime. The yearly plan renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel it in your Apple account settings.

## App Review notes

Itomi has no account or sign-in. Pro is unlocked with the in-app purchases "Pro Yearly" (com.aryan.Itomi.pro.yearly) and "Pro Lifetime" (com.aryan.Itomi.pro.lifetime). The paywall opens from the + button once there are 2 active projects, from Settings → Itomi Pro, and from the locked "Stitches per round" row when adding a part. The Lock Screen counter is turned on in Settings after buying Pro, then shows while a counter is open.
